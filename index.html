FAQs

What to do if I reach Srisailam early?
Visit nearby spots like Sakshi Ganapati Temple, Paladhara Panchadhara or the Shikhareswara viewpoint while you wait for check-in or darshan.
Is the Srisailam road open at night?
No, the Nallamala forest road is generally closed to traffic between 9:00 PM and 6:00 AM. Plan your travel during daytime, and check for extra restrictions during festivals.
What are the darshan ticket options?
Free/General Darshan is available at no cost. Paid options are Seeghra Darshan (₹150), Athi Seeghra Darshan (₹300) and Sparsha Darshan (₹500).
When is the best time to visit Srisailam?
Karthika Masam (Oct/Nov) and Maha Shivaratri (Feb/Mar) are the most significant periods, though Shivaratri sees very heavy crowds. For a quieter visit, plan outside major festival dates.
How do I get to Srisailam from Hyderabad?
TGSRTC buses run from Hyderabad (~210–220 km, 5–6 hrs) starting 2:30 AM, or by car/cab in about 4–5 hrs. YatraDham can arrange cab and package bookings — contact +91 1234567891.
